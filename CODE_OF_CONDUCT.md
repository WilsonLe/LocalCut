# Code of Conduct

LocalCut welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces, and to representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique the work rather than the person. Welcome questions, explain decisions, give actionable feedback and accept corrections. Respect privacy, accessibility needs and people's time. Credit others' work and follow the project's contribution and licensing rules.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, deliberate disruption and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Do not use community spaces for spam or unsolicited promotion.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the repository owner, [WilsonLe](https://github.com/WilsonLe), through a contact method they make available on their GitHub profile. The project does not currently publish a dedicated confidential conduct mailbox. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue asking the owner to establish one; include no allegations, names, screenshots or private information until a confidential channel is agreed. If your concern involves the owner, GitHub's own reporting tools are an available platform route.

Maintainers should limit access to reports to those needed to investigate. They should not promise absolute confidentiality, publish personal details, or require a reporter to confront the person involved. Project moderation does not replace GitHub's platform policies or reporting processes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, explain expectations privately, limit interaction, or exclude a participant from project spaces. Responses should account for severity, repetition and immediate safety. Where practical, explain the behavior at issue, the action and its duration, and allow the participant to provide context through the agreed contact channel.

Apply this policy consistently. Maintainers are subject to the same standards as contributors. There is no guaranteed response time; a concern should not be assumed resolved because it has not received a reply.
