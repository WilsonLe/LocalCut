# Workspace

- Render cached catalog summaries while revalidating; opening always reads an authoritative engine snapshot. Keep cold skeletons regional, theme-aware and reduced-motion safe. The lightweight shell/catalog may load eagerly within the bundle budget; engine, AI and heavy panels retain their lazy boundaries.
- Use the router and focused workspace hooks for route identity, current snapshot, navigation loading and saved-project catalog state. Derive the active screen from the URL; abort superseded loads before publishing. Preserve pending/error recovery, inert fresh startup and recent-project restoration at initial bare startup, and both hash-route hosting bases.
- Own view state and interaction in this directory; the editor snapshot is the canonical saved project. Route edits, history, imports, preview, and export through the public editor API.
- Submit property edits against the revision that authored the form. On conflict, refresh and let the user retry; never silently apply stale values to a newer snapshot.
- Preserve exact source endpoints for gain-only and speed-only changes. An explicit duration edit may change the source range; avoid round trips through displayed seconds for untouched values.
- Keep editor and AI imports lazy behind their existing explicit actions and the project-route/recent-project startup exceptions recorded in the parent guide. Preserve the saved-OpenRouter-credential restoration and OAuth-return exceptions and eager synchronous callback-secret removal in `oauth-callback.ts` before the lazy conversation loads.
- Keep `Conversation`, `WorkspaceDialogs`, `AIConnectionDialog`, and the connected `ConversationSession` on demand. Dialog consumers use `on-demand-dialog.tsx`: immediate cancellable opening feedback, a semantic loading shell once its optional primitives arrive, then ordinary store publication of the loaded component. Do not put first-open dialogs or their selects behind a blank Suspense retry. Preserve the opener for each closed-to-open transition, including retained dialogs, through loading and dismissal; never reopen after a cancelled load. Command metadata and palette code load concurrently on the explicit opening action; show command controls only after both are ready. Text and clip-properties bodies remain separate optional imports. Measure the full initial import graph with `pnpm check:bundle`; moving JSX alone may leave an eager dependency elsewhere.
- Keep progress, cancellation, errors, and saved revisions tied to actual jobs and receipts. Ignore superseded async results; tear down subscriptions, sessions, and artifacts with their owner.
- Keep durable chat display state and immutable canonical exchanges in `usePersistentChats.ts` through the storage chat sidecar, outside preferences/backups. Restore history only within the matching project/LLM/sharing scope; preserve completed context/tool replies and interrupted continuation through reload/forks even when index evidence changes. Historical calls never hydrate proposals, current loaded skills, asset authorization or consent. Saving failures preserve session use with retry feedback. Keep rename/archive/restore/fork in that shared owner; unmount archived chats, discard archived branch checkpoints, and preserve manual title locks.
- Collapsing chat preserves its mounted conversation and connection state. Hidden contents must be inert; keep focus usable when opening, closing, or navigating settings.
- Coordinate chat/layout transitions and respect reduced motion. Long replies scroll inside the conversation while the composer and preview remain reachable, including narrow layouts.
- Keep the last successfully opened project identity in `recent-project.ts`, outside preferences/backups; only the Workspace shell opts into consuming it on initial bare navigation; other route-hook consumers must remain passive and preserve explicit routes. OAuth callback startup leaves navigation to the existing authentication owner. Keep durable workspace choices in `preferences.ts` and appearance in its existing owner. Patch individual fields; do not persist project/session state or context authorization in preferences. Save OpenRouter credentials only in their dedicated record. Derive all text/name/transcript/capture sharing from the connected OpenRouter account, including restoration, and disclose it before connection through the [connection terms](../../docs/ai.md#openrouter-connection-terms). Keep explicit Send/Index requests. The authorized scope recovers all legacy sharing/indexing variants through the chat sidecar while retaining project/model/provider isolation; legacy removed-provider scopes must not acquire OpenRouter authority. Save deliberate layout changes, not responsive measurements or automatic panel opening. Remembered models require a connected credential and current catalog validation.
- Keep less frequent controls in the grouped settings menu. Follow the recorded [workspace preferences](../../docs/user-preferences.md) and [behavior](../../docs/workspace.md).
- Appearance shares the text-editor font catalog through its lazy font picker/adapter. Reuse the checksum-verified media loader for selected faces, prepare all subsets for future UI text, and keep pending/error/retry and stale-load guards in the appearance owner. Default startup must not load the catalog/fonts; saved bundled selections may load those display assets without editing services. Portable settings validate the complete catalog.
- Match the approved CYOBot instructor chat interaction structure while retaining LocalCut tokens. Keep media in an independently collapsible left rail and keep chat on the right and retain one AI provider settings entry point in header settings.
- Keep keyboard edits in the shared shortcut resolver. Single-key commands apply only inside the focused editor; never steal typing, IME composition, native button activation or modal/menu keys. Route playback through its existing session owner and editing shortcuts through the same revision-aware commands as visible controls.

Use the cached production UI runner for the affected scenario; for timing/revision properties:

```sh
pnpm test:ui --grep 'stale property|speed rounding'
```

For scrolling or settings changes, select the matching title in `tests/browser/workspace-regressions.spec.ts` or `workspace-local-preferences.spec.ts` (which includes the settings journey). Follow the [development loop](../../docs/development.md) for build freshness and broader gates.

- Route notifications through `notifications.ts` and errors through `showErrorToast` in `error-toast.tsx`; pass the original caught error instead of reducing it to a message. This preserves stack/session diagnostics and the persistent bell history. Keep reports local and preserve source error messages/stacks without a hardcoded safe-message allowlist; redact credentials/callback parameters. Do not read extra provider bodies, project/user content or credential stores, or initialize editor services to collect diagnostics. Use the focused notification recipe in [development](../../docs/development.md#notification-history-checks).

- Chat uses trusted requested execution without approval controls or double confirmation; keep plain text clarification for missing information, action progress/cancellation/Save and committed receipts during interrupted narration. See [the execution contract](../../docs/ai.md#workspace-requested-execution).

- `MediaFolders.tsx` owns folder navigation/forms and calls `editor.library`; freeze the library revision when opening a form. Folder upload/drop hierarchy flows through `folder-upload.ts` and the shared placement API. Preserve thumbnail-to-timeline dragging and offer context-menu move alternatives. See [Media folders](../../docs/workspace.md#media-folders).

- Keep AI connection binary: OpenRouter connected or disconnected. Normalize legacy settings to one retained account and one model/voice per service; keep its credential namespace and discard backups. Local Whisper remains an explicit STT choice; failed local/remote work never switches services.

- Composer references accept ready local images/audio/videos through selection, native drop and Media drag. Keep stable attachment IDs through draft reload/Retry, use the shared asset importer and leave timeline placement/inference to explicit requests. Native workspace upload routing must leave composer drops to that owner. See [reference workflow](../../docs/workspace.md#audio-and-video-references-in-chat).
